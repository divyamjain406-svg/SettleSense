# Procedure PROC_14: Multi-Factor Exception Escalation and Ambiguous Narrative Resolution

- **Procedure ID:** PROC_14
- **Title:** Multi-Factor Exception Escalation and Ambiguous Narrative Resolution
- **Relevant Category:** UNDETERMINED
- **Source Type:** Synthetic / Illustrative Operational Guidance

## Trigger Indicators
- Narrative presents competing or contradictory indicators (e.g., simultaneous mention of cash deficit and counterparty dispute).
- Operational report conflates multiple interdependent trade legs with conflicting failure statuses.
- Insufficient contextual evidence to distinguish between a matching breakdown and a timing delay.

## Evidence to Inspect
- Multi-party correspondence thread (emails, chat logs, dealer notes).
- End-to-end transaction lifecycle timeline across execution, matching, clearing, and depository layers.
- Conflicting status records between central matching utility and depository clearing agent.

## Generic Verification Steps
1. Dissect composite exception narratives into discrete, chronologically ordered event phases.
2. Separate observed objective transactional facts from analyst speculation or secondary symptoms.
3. Determine which event occurred first (e.g., whether a counterparty mismatch caused late submission, or whether late submission led to cash cutoff).
4. Direct targeted bilateral inquiries to specific operational contacts (middle office, treasury, custodian) to resolve each ambiguous factor.
5. Apply interim risk status to protect client positions while root cause is being investigated.

## Missing Information to Collect
- Clarification from executing broker regarding primary operational bottleneck.
- Authenticated status statement from settlement clearing agent resolving contradictory codes.

## Conditions for Human Escalation
- Resolution requires executive operational or legal consensus due to ambiguous contractual terms.
- Conflicting claims between counterparties exceed standard operational dispute procedures.
